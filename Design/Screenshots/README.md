# App Store screenshots

Owner-supplied screenshots for the Mac App Store. Both PNGs are 2880 × 1800, RGB, without alpha.

The exports were normalized from 2800 × 1800: 25 pixels of wallpaper were trimmed from the top and bottom, then the result was resized proportionally. App content was retained. Store order: `padpad-markdown.png`, then `padpad-thoughts.png`.
